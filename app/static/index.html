    Every scene, extracted and ranked.

    Upload a video and ClipRanker splits it into scenes, cuts each scene out as its own clip, and ranks the clips from most to least important. It also builds a short highlight reel.

    1. ShotsTransNetV2 finds every cut at the video's real frame rate
    2. SpeechWhisper transcribes what is said
    3. Scenesshots are grouped by what they show (CLIP) and what is said (Sentence-BERT)
    4. Clipseach scene is cut out as an MP4 clip
    5. Rankinga model trained on the TVSum benchmark scores each clip, together with audio and dialogue cues

    Videos up to 10 minutes. Your uploads are private to this browser and deleted automatically after a day.

      Run ClipRanker on your own video
      
      
      The examples on this page were processed ahead of time. To process your own video,
        run ClipRanker in one of these two ways. Both are free.
      

      
      
        
      1. In your browser, with a free Google GPU
      
        
      Open the notebook in Google Colab, choose Runtime, Change runtime type, T4 GPU, then
          Runtime, Run all. After about three minutes of setup, the full ClipRanker app opens
          inside Colab and you can upload any video.
      
        Open in Colab
      
      

      
      
        
      2. On your own computer
      
        
      Needs Python 3.10 or newer. An NVIDIA GPU makes it about ten times faster.
      

      git clone 
cd Semantic-Video-Synthesis-with-BERT
pip install torch torchvision --index-url https://download.pytorch.org/whl/cu121
pip install -r requirements.txt
python scripts/download_weights.py
python -m app.server
      
        
      Then open http://127.0.0.1:8000 and upload a video.
