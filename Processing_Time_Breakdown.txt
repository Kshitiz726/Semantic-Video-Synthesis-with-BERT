Performance Bottlenecks:
  1. Stage 8 (Importance Scoring) - 33.3% of total time
     → CLIP feature extraction on all video frames
     → Whisper ASR transcription per clip
     → Audio spectral analysis
  
  2. Stage 1 (Shot Detection + ASR) - 26.7% of total time
     → TransNetV2 inference on full video
     → Initial Whisper transcription

Optimization Suggestions:
  • Use GPU acceleration for CLIP and Whisper (reduces time by ~50%)
  • Cache intermediate results to skip stages during re-runs
  • Use lower resolution for visual features (224×224 → 128×128)
  • Process multiple clips in parallel during Stage 8
